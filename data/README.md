# ESS source data

These are unchanged integrated ESS releases, not model predictions or study results.
The originals are freely available from the official [ESS Data Portal](https://ess.sikt.no/).
The specific editions and SHA-256 checksums are recorded in `sources.json`.

- European Social Survey European Research Infrastructure (ESS ERIC). (2023).
  *ESS Round 7 integrated file edition 2.3*. Sikt. https://doi.org/10.21338/ess7e02_3
- European Social Survey European Research Infrastructure (ESS ERIC). (2026).
  *ESS Round 11 integrated file edition 4.2*. Sikt. https://doi.org/10.21338/ess11e04_2
